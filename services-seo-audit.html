What is an SEO audit?

An SEO audit is a structured review of a website's search engine optimization. It shows why the site ranks where it does and what to change first. It covers technical SEO, on-page SEO, content, links and user experience, and compares your site with the pages that rank above you.

A useful audit does not end with 200 warnings from a tool. It ranks the issues by their impact on visibility and inquiries, so you know where your budget makes a difference.

What we check in your free SEO analysis

01/ 07

  1. 01

    Technical SEO

    We crawl your website with Screaming Frog and check Google Search Console if you give us access. We look at indexing, broken pages, redirects, canonical tags, the XML sitemap and robots.txt. We also test mobile speed with Lighthouse and PageSpeed Insights, including the Core Web Vitals.

  2. 02

    On-page SEO and content

    We review titles, meta descriptions, headings and internal links on your most important pages. We check whether each service has its own page and whether it matches what people in Dubai actually search. Thin, duplicate or outdated content gets a clear recommendation.

  3. 03

    Visibility in Google and Google Maps

    We check your rankings from a Dubai location, not from abroad. For local businesses, we review your Google Business Profile, categories, reviews and the consistency of your listings. This is often where the quickest gains are.

  4. 04

    Links pointing to your site

    We analyze your backlink profile with Ahrefs and Semrush. How many sites link to you, how relevant are they, and are there spammy patterns? We compare your links with those of the competitors who rank above you.

  5. 05

    Competitor analysis

    Our competitor analysis shows who ranks for your main services in Dubai and why. We compare speed, content depth, reviews and links, so you see the gap in concrete terms.

  6. 06

    Usability and inquiries

    Rankings only matter if visitors become inquiries. We check the user experience on mobile: how easy it is to call, message on WhatsApp or send a form. We point out obstacles that lower your conversion rate, such as slow pages, hidden contact details or long forms.

  7. 07

    AI visibility

    We test whether Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity mention your business for typical questions in your field. In our own tests in October 2026, these tools based their answers on reviews, directories and clear facts on company websites. We show you which of these sources you are missing.

FAQ about SEO audits

How much does an SEO audit cost in Dubai and the UAE?

The cost of SEO audit services in the UAE depends on the size of the website and the depth of the analysis. We start with a free SEO analysis for UAE businesses. An in-depth technical SEO audit with an implementation plan is quoted individually, based on your site, your goals and the scope of work. You can see how our SEO packages work.

Can ChatGPT do an SEO audit?

ChatGPT is helpful for checklists, explaining SEO terms and reviewing text you paste into it. It can read single pages, but it cannot crawl your whole site, see your Search Console data or check rankings from a Dubai location. Use it as a helper, not as a replacement for a real audit.

Do I need to give you access to my accounts?

No. We can run the free analysis with public data. Read-only access to Google Search Console and GA4 makes the results more precise, but it is optional.

Is there any obligation after the free SEO analysis?

No. You keep the written summary and can use it however you like. We do not add you to an automated sales sequence.

Can you audit a website that is not live yet?

Yes, if we can access a staging version. Checking a new site before launch is often the cheapest way to avoid ranking losses. For relaunches, we also review the redirect plan from the old website.
