What is link building?

Link building is the work of earning links from other websites to yours, so that search engines treat your pages as trustworthy and relevant. A link from a respected UAE business publication says more than a hundred links from unrelated sites. Quality and relevance count, not volume.

SEO content writing is the craft of writing pages that match search intent: what a person actually wants when they type a query. The two belong together. Good content gives other sites a reason to link, and links help good content rank.

  • Good content
  • A reason to link
  • Links help good content rank

SEO content writing for UAE businesses

  1. 01

    UAE keyword research

    We research how your buyers in the UAE search, not how a global keyword tool assumes they do. We use Semrush, SISTRIX, Ahrefs, Google Search Console and the questions Google shows in "People also ask". The result is a content plan that assigns one main keyword and one clear purpose to every page.

  2. 02

    Service and landing pages

    Service and landing pages carry most of your inquiries. We write them to answer the buyer's real questions: what you offer, for whom, where, at what price range and how the process works. Each page has one call to action, so readers know what to do next.

  3. 03

    Guides that answer real questions

    Guides and FAQ pages bring visitors who are still researching. They also get cited by AI tools: in the SISTRIX AI Check, agency domains appeared mainly for informational questions. That makes guides part of our AI search optimization work as well.

  4. 04

    Author, date and facts on every page

    Every page we write names an author, shows a date and states facts you can check. Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page and why. We answer both questions openly on the page itself.

Authority: how we earn links and mentions in Dubai

  1. 01

    Digital PR with data

    Journalists and editors link to original data, clear opinions and useful tools. We help you turn what you already know into a story: a survey of your customers, a price index or a benchmark. We test this approach on ourselves first, for example with our own speed test of 15 Dubai SEO agency websites in October 2026.

  2. 02

    Directories that matter in the UAE

    Directories still matter, but only the right ones. When we asked Perplexity about SEO agencies in Dubai, it cited mainly Clutch, Semrush Agency Partners, DesignRush and Sortlist. Your industry has its own equivalents, such as specialist portals, association lists and booking platforms. We find the ones that rank and get cited for your topics, then build complete profiles there.

  3. 03

    Partner and supplier mentions

    Your suppliers, partners, associations and event organizers often list the companies they work with. These links are relevant, earned and easy to verify. We collect them systematically instead of leaving them to chance.

  4. 04

    Recovering mentions and broken links

    Many businesses already get mentioned online without a link. We find these unlinked mentions and ask the publisher to add a link. We also check for links that point to pages you deleted, then redirect or restore those pages so the value isn't lost.

What we refuse to do

We don't buy links, rent links or use private blog networks (PBNs). These tactics count as link spam under Google's spam policies, and they can cost a site its rankings. Paid placements are fine for visibility, but they must be marked as sponsored and never sold to you as SEO.

We also don't produce mass content to fill a quota. A page goes live when it answers a question better than what already ranks.

How we measure content and authority

We track rankings for each page's main keyword, organic traffic and the inquiries those pages bring in. For authority, the core figure is the number of relevant referring domains, meaning distinct websites that link to you. We pull this data from Ahrefs, Semrush and Google Search Console.

  • Rankings for each page's main keyword
  • Organic traffic
  • The inquiries those pages bring in
  • Relevant referring domains

Authority Score (Semrush) and Domain Rating (Ahrefs) are useful as context when you compare yourself with competitors. We don't treat them as targets, because Google doesn't use them.

FAQ: SEO content and link building

How many backlinks do I need to rank in Dubai?

There is no fixed number. It depends on how many relevant sites link to the pages that currently rank for your keywords. We compare your link profile with those competitors and set a realistic target from that gap.

Do you buy links or use PBNs?

No. Buying links that pass ranking value and using private blog networks both break Google's spam policies. If we ever recommend a paid placement for visibility, we say so openly and mark the link as sponsored.

Does link building still matter with AI search?

Yes. AI tools that search the web start from pages that already rank and from sources they consider trustworthy. Mentions on respected sites help in both places, even without a link.

Who writes the content, and do we approve it?

Our team writes the content in English, based on input from you and your team. Nothing goes live without your approval. You own every page we write, even if we stop working together.

Is a directory profile worth it for my business?

Some are, most aren't. A profile pays off if the directory ranks for your services or gets cited by AI tools. Buyers in your industry should also actually use it, and we check both before we create one.
