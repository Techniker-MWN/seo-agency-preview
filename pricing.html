What drives the cost of SEO in Dubai

The cost of SEO is the result of the work your market requires, not of your company size. Seven factors decide how much work that is.

  1. 01

    Competition

    A clinic in a busy district competes with many practices that already invest in SEO. A specialized B2B supplier may face only a handful of serious competitors. The stronger the competition, the more content, links and time you need.

  2. 02

    Number of locations

    Every branch needs its own Google Business Profile, location page, citations and review process. The local work grows with each location, while strategy and reporting stay shared.

  3. 03

    Number of services and pages

    Each additional service or target market adds keyword research, pages and monitoring. A business with two services needs a smaller plan than one with fifteen.

  4. 04

    Technical state of your website

    A fast, clean website needs little technical work. A slow site with indexing errors or a pending relaunch needs development time first. Our free SEO analysis shows which case applies to you.

  5. 05

    Content volume

    New service pages, landing pages and guides take research, writing and editing. The more pages your market expects, the higher the regular effort.

  6. 06

    Link building and digital PR

    Some markets reward a solid citation base. Others require editorial mentions in relevant UAE publications, which take outreach and real stories to earn.

  7. 07

    Scope of AI search work

    Visibility in Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity depends on clear facts, structured data and presence on the sources these tools cite. A quarterly check is enough for some businesses. Others need monthly monitoring and active work on those sources.

What every proposal from us contains

Every proposal comes in writing, so you can compare it line by line. It always includes:

  • the package or project scope, with the work we do each month
  • the deliverables, for example pages, technical fixes, citations and links
  • the reporting format, the KPIs we track and how often we hold review calls
  • the people who work on your account
  • the fee and what it covers
  • the contract term, renewal and notice period
  • a clear statement that your content, accounts and data stay yours

If something in the proposal is unclear, we explain it before you sign. Nothing important lives only in a phone call.

What is not included

These costs sit outside our fee, and we name them upfront:

FAQ about SEO pricing in Dubai

How much does SEO cost in Dubai?

It depends on your competition, the number of locations and services, the technical state of your website and the content volume your market needs. That is why we don't quote a figure in advance. We prepare an individual offer after your free SEO analysis, based on your goals, your competition and the scope of work.

Why don't you publish prices?

Because a published price would be wrong for most businesses that read it. A single-location salon and an online shop with thousands of products need very different work. A clear, written offer after an analysis is more honest than a price that changes once we look at your website.

What is the 80/20 rule for SEO?

The 80/20 rule, or Pareto principle, says that a small share of causes produces most of the results. In SEO, a few pages, keywords and fixes usually bring most of the traffic and inquiries. We apply it by starting every plan with the changes that have the biggest expected impact.

Can I start small and scale up?

Yes. Many businesses start with Local or a one-off project and move to Growth or Authority once the first results justify it. We review the scope with you regularly and recommend changes only when your market requires them.

How do I compare your proposal with other offers?

Compare the scope, not just the fee. Check which deliverables, how much content and which reporting each offer includes, and who does the work. Ask every agency how the contract term and renewal work, and get it in writing.
