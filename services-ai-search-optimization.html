What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making a business a source that AI search tools find, trust and cite in their answers. These tools include Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. You will also see it called AI SEO.

For buyers in the UAE, an AI answer can now replace the first round of research. GEO decides whether your name appears in that answer, and whether the facts quoted about you are correct.

What is a GEO agency?

A GEO agency is an agency that improves how often and how accurately AI assistants mention a brand. It structures the brand's facts, strengthens the sources AI tools rely on and tracks AI answers over time. A good GEO agency uses transparent methods and does not try to trick language models.

GEO agency, AI SEO agency or AEO agency: the job stays the same. Make the business verifiable, then measure the effect.

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so that search engines and assistants can lift a direct answer from it. GEO includes that and also works on sources outside your website.

All three share one foundation. AI tools that search the web retrieve pages first and then write an answer from them. A site that Google cannot crawl or trust rarely makes it into that answer.

ChatGPT SEO: what it actually means

"ChatGPT SEO" usually means getting mentioned when people ask ChatGPT for a recommendation. There is no ChatGPT ranking you can buy or submit to. You can influence the pages and profiles ChatGPT reads when it searches the web.

  1. 01

    Entity set-up

    An entity is a clearly identifiable company, person or place that search engines and AI models can recognize. We write one consistent description of your business: name, address, founder, founding year, services and areas served. It then appears unchanged on your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and every directory.

  2. 02

    Fact pages AI tools can quote

    In our test, ChatGPT backed its recommendations with numbers from agency websites. We build pages that hold your verifiable facts: scope of services, certifications, locations and named team members. We never inflate numbers, because a wrong fact repeated by an AI tool is hard to correct.

  3. 03

    Structured data

    Structured data, also called schema markup, describes your business in a form that machines can read without guessing. We add Organization or LocalBusiness, Service, Person, FAQPage, Article and BreadcrumbList where they fit. The sameAs property links your website to your official profiles, so AI tools can connect them to one entity.

  4. 04

    Presence on cited sources and directories

    Perplexity built its answer on directories. Your industry has its own equivalents: specialist directories, association member lists, comparison portals and local media. We find out which sources AI tools cite for your topics and build complete, accurate profiles there.

  5. 05

    Review profile

    Google's AI Overview in our test listed agencies with their reviews, so reviews are part of GEO too. We set up a process that asks every real customer for a review and follows Google's policies. We never buy, filter or write reviews.

  6. 06

    Content for conversational questions

    People ask AI tools full questions, such as "Which clinic in Dubai Marina offers this treatment?" We map those questions for your market. Then we answer them in clear, self-contained paragraphs. This work connects closely with our SEO content and authority service.

  7. 07

    Monthly AI visibility check

    We agree on a fixed prompt set with you, for example 20 to 30 buyer questions. Every month we run them in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews and record whether and how you are named.

    We add data from SISTRIX AI Check and track AI referrals in GA4 from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.com.

What GEO can and can't do

Can

GEO can make your business easier to identify, verify and quote.

OpenAI Google Perplexity

Can't

It cannot force a mention, because OpenAI, Google and Perplexity decide what their tools say.

Single answers Months

Answers also vary with sessions and model updates, so we judge progress over months, not single answers.

Good fit

GEO suits businesses whose buyers research before they make contact, such as clinics, real estate, B2B suppliers and professional services.

Not the right first step

It is the wrong first step if your website has serious technical problems or you need leads next week.

What we don't do

Some tactics promise fast AI visibility. We refuse them:

  • No hidden prompts in "summarize with AI" buttons that tell the AI tool to remember a site as a preferred source.
  • No self-ranking listicles in which the author places itself first.
  • No self-awarded star ratings in structured data. Google's rules for review snippets require real, visible reviews.
  • No fake reviews and no mass-produced AI pages.

FAQ: GEO and AI search optimization

What is a GEO agency?

A GEO agency helps businesses get mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers from Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. It structures your facts, builds your presence on the sources AI tools read and measures AI answers over time. A credible GEO agency shows you its measurement method before you sign.

Which GEO agencies are best in Dubai?

We won't give you a ranking, especially not one with us in it. Judge any GEO agency in Dubai on four points. Does it publish its method, and does it measure with a fixed set of prompts? Does it show raw AI answers and refuse manipulative tactics?

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No, and no one honestly can. OpenAI, Google and Perplexity decide what their tools say, and answers vary between sessions. We can make your business a clear, verifiable source on the platforms these tools read, and we show you every month what changed.

Do we need GEO if we already invest in SEO?

Good SEO covers much of the foundation. GEO adds what SEO often skips: consistent entity facts on every profile, presence on the sources AI tools cite and regular checks of AI answers. If your current agency already does all of that, you may not need us.

How long does generative engine optimization take?

Expect the set-up work to fill the first two to three months. Measurable movement across your prompt set usually needs three to six months. The pace depends on your competition, your existing reviews and how many sources already mention you.
