What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the work of making a website easy for search engines to crawl, render, index and understand, and fast and stable for visitors. It covers the infrastructure behind your content: server responses, site structure, code, speed and structured data.

Good content and strong links only pay off when this foundation works. In Dubai, sites are often rebuilt quickly, so broken redirects and slow mobile pages are common.

What we check and fix

01/ 08

  1. 01

    Crawling and crawl budget

    We crawl your site with Screaming Frog, compare the results with Google Search Console and find blocked, orphaned or broken pages. On larger sites, we check whether crawl budget is wasted on filters, parameters or duplicate URLs.

  2. 02

    Indexing, XML sitemap and robots.txt

    We check which pages Google has indexed and why others are excluded. Your XML sitemap should list only the pages that matter, and robots.txt should block only what must stay out.

  3. 03

    Canonical tags and redirects

    We set canonical tags so Google knows which version counts. We also remove redirect chains, loops and broken links, and give every valuable old URL a direct 301 redirect.

  4. 04

    Structured data

    Structured data in Schema.org format helps Google and AI systems understand who you are and what you offer. We add and validate Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, Product and BreadcrumbList markup where it fits.

  5. 05

    Mobile-first indexing and JavaScript rendering

    Google uses the mobile version of your website for indexing, so content missing on mobile is effectively missing. We test how Googlebot renders your pages and whether key content depends on JavaScript. Sites built with heavy JavaScript frameworks often show search engines an empty page at first.

  6. 06

    Core Web Vitals: LCP, INP and CLS

    Core Web Vitals are Google's metrics for loading, responsiveness and visual stability. Google rates Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) up to 2.5 seconds as good. For Interaction to Next Paint (INP) the limit is 200 milliseconds, for Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) 0.1.

    Our website speed optimization covers images, fonts, scripts, caching and server response times.

  7. 07

    Log file analysis

    Server log files show exactly which URLs Google requests and how often. On large sites, log file analysis reveals pages that Google ignores and bots that waste crawl activity.

  8. 08

    Migrations, relaunches and hreflang

    A relaunch without a redirect plan can wipe out years of rankings. We map every old URL, test the new site before launch and monitor Search Console afterwards. For sites with several language or country versions, we set up and test hreflang so Google shows the right version to the right users.

Who technical SEO is for

Technical SEO matters most for:

  • websites with many pages, such as online shops, property portals and directories
  • businesses planning a redesign, a new CMS or a domain change
  • sites that lost traffic after a Google update or a relaunch
  • websites that are slow on mobile or built mainly with JavaScript

For a small site on a modern system, a one-off check is often enough. If a rebuild makes more sense than repairs, see our SEO-ready websites.

FAQ about technical SEO

What does technical SEO do?

Technical SEO makes sure search engines can crawl, render, index and understand your website. It also makes pages fast and stable for visitors. Without it, even strong content may never appear in Google, because search engines cannot reach or process it properly.

Is SEO replaced by AI?

No. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity build their answers from content they can crawl and trust. That makes technical SEO more important, not less. Many AI crawlers read the raw HTML and do not render JavaScript reliably, so content hidden behind scripts can stay invisible to them.

Do you work with Shopify, WordPress and October CMS?

Yes, and with WooCommerce and most custom builds. Some platforms limit what can be changed, for example the fixed URL structure on Shopify. We explain those limits before we start.

Will a website migration lose my rankings?

Every migration carries some risk, but a careful plan keeps it small. We map every URL, set redirects and test the new site before launch. Many ranking losses come from forgotten redirects, which is exactly what we prevent.

How long do technical fixes take to show results?

Indexing fixes can show effects once Google recrawls the pages, often within weeks. Speed improvements appear in Google's field data after about 28 days, its rolling measurement window.

Do you need access to our server or CMS?

Not for the free check. For implementation, we need access to your CMS and Search Console, sometimes also to hosting. You keep ownership of all accounts.
