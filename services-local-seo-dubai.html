What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of making a business appear in Google Maps and the local search results when people nearby look for its services. It combines your Google Business Profile, consistent listings across the web, location pages on your website and genuine customer reviews.

In Dubai, a search for a dentist in Jumeirah shows different practices from the same search in Deira. Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, so we work on all three.

What our local SEO services include

01/ 05

  1. 01Your Google Business Profile
  2. 02Listings and citations
  3. 03Location and service pages
  4. 04Reviews that follow Google's rules
  5. 05Tracking calls, WhatsApp clicks and directions

  1. 01

    Your Google Business Profile

    Your Google Business Profile is the most important asset in local search. We set up or take over your profile and complete it: categories, services, hours, service areas, photos and description.

    Business profile optimization continues every month with posts, photos and updates whenever your offer changes.

  2. 02

    Listings and citations

    A citation is any mention of your business name, address and phone number on another website. We correct wrong or duplicate entries and add you to relevant UAE and industry directories. Citation management keeps these details identical, which helps Google trust your business.

  3. 03

    Location and service pages

    We create or improve pages for your main services and, where it makes sense, for the areas you serve. Each page answers real customer questions and carries structured data, so it can also rank in organic local search below the map.

  4. 04

    Reviews that follow Google's rules

    Reviews influence both your local rankings and the decision to call you. We set up a simple process for asking every real customer for a review, for example by QR code, WhatsApp or email after a visit.

    We never buy, fake, filter or incentivize reviews, because this breaks Google's policies and can get a profile suspended.

  5. 05

    Tracking calls, WhatsApp clicks and directions

    We set up tracking with Google Tag Manager and GA4, alongside the performance data in your profile. You see how many people called, clicked to WhatsApp or asked for directions.

Who local SEO is for

Local SEO suits any business whose customers search by area or want a provider close to them. Typical examples are:

  • clinics, dentists, restaurants and salons
  • home services such as cleaning and AC repair
  • real estate brokers, car services and professional firms
Good fit

It pays off when customers are actively searching for services in Dubai and want to call or visit soon.

Not the right fit

It is not the right fit if you sell only online, without a location or service area. Google's guidelines also exclude unstaffed virtual offices.

FAQ about local SEO in Dubai

How long until we appear in the top three on the map?

Profile fixes and category changes can show effects within a few weeks. Reaching the top three for competitive Dubai searches usually takes several months. Progress depends on your category, your area, your competitors and the number of genuine reviews you collect.

Can you guarantee a top-three position on Google Maps?

No. Google decides the rankings, and the distance between a searcher and your business is outside anyone's control. We commit to doing the work properly and to showing you transparent data every month.

Can you get us more reviews quickly?

We help you ask every real customer for a review in a simple, consistent way. We never buy, fake or incentivize reviews. Google's policies prohibit this, and it can lead to deleted reviews or a suspended profile.

We have several branches. Does local SEO work for each one?

Yes. Each branch gets its own Google Business Profile, location page and tracking, and we report per location.

Can we rank in areas where we have no office?

In the map, distance to the searcher limits this, even if you set a wide service area. In the regular results, well-written area and service pages can rank across Dubai. We show you where each approach is realistic for your business.
