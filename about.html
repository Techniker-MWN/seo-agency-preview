Why we exist

The SEO market in Dubai is loud. Many agencies promise top positions, publish star ratings without a source or rank themselves first in their own lists. For a business owner, it is hard to tell who actually knows the work.

We started SEO Agency AE to be the opposite. We are clear about what we do and honest about what SEO can and cannot deliver. We also stand behind every number we report, because we show you where it comes from.

Our background

Our team has more than 20 years of SEO experience, gained mainly in Germany. German search markets are crowded and demanding, so we learned to win with clean technology, useful content and patient work. Our team has carried out more than 500 SEO audits.

We brought that experience to Dubai in 2025. The standards stay the same here: clean technology, useful content and numbers you can check.

  • 20+ more than 20 years of SEO experience
  • 500+ more than 500 SEO audits
  • a lab test of 15 top-ranking Dubai SEO agency websites

Our own research shows how we work. In October 2026, for example, we ran a lab test of 15 top-ranking Dubai SEO agency websites with Lighthouse 12 on mobile. Only 1 of the 15 reached Google's "good" loading threshold of 2.5 seconds. That is the kind of measured, checkable insight you can expect from us.

How we work

Four principles guide every project, from a small local business to a larger company website.

  1. 01

    You work with the people who do the work.

    The specialists who plan your strategy join every review call. There is no hand-off to a junior account manager.

  2. 02

    Implementation, not just advice.

    Our developers build and fix, and our writers write. Changes go live in days rather than months.

  3. 03

    Transparent reporting.

    Every month, you see what we did, why we did it and what it changed. Our reports draw on GA4, Google Search Console and rank tracking from a Dubai location.

  4. 04

    Ownership stays with you.

    Your content, accounts and data belong to you. If you ever leave, you keep everything.

We work in English. Our main tools are Google Search Console, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Screaming Frog, Semrush, SISTRIX, Ahrefs and PageSpeed Insights. We build websites in-house on October CMS, WordPress, Shopify and WooCommerce.

What we don't do

Some things we refuse to do, even when a client asks for them. They harm your business in the long run and break the rules of Google and other platforms.

01/ 05

  1. 01

    No ranking promises.

    Google decides its rankings, and nobody can promise a fixed position.

  2. 02

    No bought links or private blog networks.

    They violate Google's spam policies and put your site at risk.

  3. 03

    No fake reviews.

    We help you collect real reviews from real customers, in line with Google's rules.

  4. 04

    No invented results.

    Anything we publish about results, clients or ratings must be real and verifiable.

  5. 05

    No self-ranking lists.

    We will never publish a "top agencies" list with ourselves at the top.

We also tell you when SEO is not the right tool. If paid ads, a better offer or a faster reply process would help you sooner, we say so.
